# Engagement Letter (Master) — Standard Tax Preparation
*(service code: `TAX-PREP` — the standing letter every client signs during onboarding)*

This is the foundational document every addendum in `docs/pandadoc-addenda/`
refers back to by client name and date. Everything downstream — the
BOOKKEEPING/CLEANUP/ANNUAL-REPORT/SALES-TAX addenda, the annual
re-engagement checkpoint — assumes this letter exists and was signed once
per client per year.

---

**ENGAGEMENT LETTER**
**Jehovah Jireh Tax Consultants**
*Your Vision Is Our Mission.*

This Engagement Letter ("Agreement") is entered into by and between
Jehovah Jireh Tax Consultants ("JJTC," "we," "us," "our") and
**[Client Full Name]** / **[Business Name, if applicable]** ("Client,"
"you," "your"), effective as of **[Date]**.

**1. Scope of Services**

JJTC will prepare Client's **[Tax Year]** federal and state income tax
return(s) based on information and documents Client provides. This
engagement covers tax preparation only. It does not include bookkeeping,
records cleanup, annual report renewals, sales tax filings, audit
representation, or any other service unless separately identified in a
signed addendum to this Agreement (see Section 5).

**2. Client Responsibilities**

Client agrees to:

- Submit all documents (receipts, statements, prior returns, 1099s,
  correspondence, and any other records JJTC requests) through Client's
  Financial Cents client portal. **Documents sent by email or text are not
  considered received and will not begin any turnaround timeline.**
- Provide complete and accurate information. JJTC is not responsible for
  errors resulting from incomplete or inaccurate information Client
  provides.
- Respond to requests for clarification or missing documents in a timely
  manner, as delays on Client's part will delay the engagement.

**3. JJTC's Responsibilities**

JJTC will prepare the return(s) described in Section 1 using professional
judgment and applicable tax law, and will notify Client of any additional
information needed to complete the engagement.

**4. Fees & Payment**

Fees for the services described in Section 1 are **[fee structure — flat
fee $____ / hourly at $____ / billed upon completion]**. Payment is due
**[upon signing / upon completion, before filing]**. JJTC reserves the
right to withhold filing until payment is received in full.

**5. Additional Services**

This Agreement covers the specific services listed in Section 1 only. Any
additional work — including bookkeeping, records cleanup, annual report
renewals, or additional tax filings — requires a signed addendum and
payment before work begins. This protects both of us: you know exactly
what you're paying for, and we can give your file the attention it needs
without delay.

**6. Term & Renewal**

This engagement covers tax year **[Tax Year]** only. Continuing services
in a subsequent year require a new signed engagement letter for that year.

**7. Confidentiality**

JJTC will maintain the confidentiality of Client's information in
accordance with applicable law and professional standards, and will not
disclose it to third parties except as required to perform the services
described above or as required by law.

**8. Limitation of Liability**

JJTC's liability for any claim arising from this engagement is limited to
the fees paid by Client for the services giving rise to the claim. JJTC is
not liable for penalties or interest resulting from Client's failure to
provide timely or accurate information.

**9. Acknowledgment**

By signing below, Client acknowledges having read and agreed to the terms
of this Agreement, including the document-submission and additional-scope
policies in Sections 2 and 5.
