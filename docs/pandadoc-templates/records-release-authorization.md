# Records Release Authorization
*(used only in the "Switching Accountants" onboarding path, before the master Engagement Letter)*

Authorizes the release of Client's records from their prior accountant and
confirms there's nothing left open with that prior firm before JJTC takes
over — matching the description already shown on this step in the app
(`situations.py`'s `RECORDS_RELEASE_ESIGN`).

---

**RECORDS RELEASE AUTHORIZATION**
**Jehovah Jireh Tax Consultants**
*Your Vision Is Our Mission.*

I, **[Client Full Name]**, authorize the release of the following to
Jehovah Jireh Tax Consultants ("JJTC"):

- All passwords and login credentials for accounts related to my tax and
  financial records held by my prior accountant/firm, **[Prior Accountant
  / Firm Name]**
- All files, documents, and records related to my tax and financial
  affairs currently held by the above prior accountant/firm
- Power of Attorney (Form 2848 or equivalent) currently on file naming the
  above prior accountant/firm, which I intend to revoke and replace with a
  new Power of Attorney naming JJTC

**Confirmation of Closed Items**

I confirm that, to the best of my knowledge, there are no open items,
outstanding fees, or unresolved matters with **[Prior Accountant / Firm
Name]** that would prevent a clean transfer of my records to JJTC. If any
such item exists, I have disclosed it to JJTC below:

**[Disclosed open items, if any — otherwise "None"]**

**Acknowledgment**

I understand that JJTC's ability to begin work is contingent on receiving
the records described above, and that any delay in their release by my
prior accountant/firm may delay my engagement with JJTC. This
authorization does not itself constitute an engagement letter; a separate
Engagement Letter governs the scope of JJTC's services.
